Our Purpose and Mission

Our goal is to raise awareness about the protection of natural water bodies worldwide, from freshwater rivers to oceanic seas. Established on January 15, 2026, our organization works to educate the public on environmental sustainability.

We believe that protecting aquatic biodiversity is necessary for sustaining future life on Earth.

"Water is the driving force of all nature."

We focus on pollution prevention, riverbed restoration, marine life preservation, and sustainable community water use.
