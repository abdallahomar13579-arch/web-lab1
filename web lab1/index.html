Rivers: The Lifelines of Our Planet

Rivers are important natural sources of water that flow from higher areas toward seas, lakes, or oceans. They provide water for people, agriculture, and animals, and they are home to many different plants and species. Rivers have also played an important role in human history, as many ancient civilizations developed near them. Today, protecting rivers from pollution and excessive water use is essential for both people and nature.

The Importance of Seas

Seas are large bodies of salt water that are usually smaller than oceans and often partly surrounded by land. They are home to many different kinds of plants and animals, including fish, dolphins, turtles, and coral reefs. Seas also play an important role in regulating the Earth's climate by absorbing and storing heat. Humans depend on seas for fishing, transportation, tourism, and other activities. However, pollution, overfishing, and climate change are threatening marine ecosystems. Protecting seas is important for maintaining healthy oceans and supporting life on Earth.
